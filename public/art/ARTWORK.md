# Background artwork

The original `blue-mountains.png` remains unchanged.

`misty-forest.png` and `moonlit-coast.png` were created for Quiet Field on 2026-09-22 using OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. They are visual artwork, separate from the audio library. Both files are stored locally and require no network connection at runtime.

## Misty forest prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium desktop ambience-app wallpaper, wide 16:9 landscape 2048x1152. Create an exquisitely tranquil deep blue misty evergreen forest at night, rich photographic matte-painting detail, layers of fir trees receding into blue haze and a distant mountain ridge. A modest pale moon glow diffused in the upper right, no visible bright moon necessary. Calm mist rests low between trees. Compose all the landscape detail across the bottom half and far right; leave the upper left and center smooth deep navy negative space so white app text remains readable. Dark, restrained monochrome indigo and glacial-blue palette, soft low-key natural lighting, cinematic but unshowy. Full bleed single wallpaper, no text, no interface, no borders, no people, no buildings, no neon, no purple, no watermarks. Save the final generated image as a local file for use as an application asset.

## Moonlit coast prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium desktop ambience-app wallpaper, wide 16:9 landscape 2048x1152. Create a serene moonlit blue coastline: smooth dark ocean with delicate silver-blue wavelets, a long low distant rocky headland on the far right, atmospheric depth, a small pale crescent moon high toward the upper right and a restrained narrow reflection on the water below. Horizon across lower half, ocean below, soft clear navy sky above. Elegant photographic matte-painting with beautifully subtle realistic water texture, timeless and quiet. Keep center and left dark, low contrast and uncluttered for overlaying white app text. Palette deep midnight navy, muted slate blue and very restrained icy blue. No orange, no purple, no neon, no big bright moon, no ships, no buildings, no people, no text, no interface, no borders, no watermark. Full bleed single wallpaper. Save the final generated image as a local file for use as an application asset.

## Code-based scenes

Midnight, deep ocean and graphite are solid colors. Quiet aurora and starlit night are CSS gradients with optional slow motion. They use no remote assets, video, canvas or animation loop in JavaScript.
